Mathematics 数学

Sets and inequalities, functions, trigonometry, sequences, derivatives, vectors and complex numbers, analytic and solid geometry, probability and statistics.

The real exam: 48 single-choice questions in 60 minutes.

Work through the topics in order: later topics use earlier ones. Inside a topic, do the tasks before you read the rules.

  1. 1Sets and inequalities集合与不等式

    Sets and subsets, ∩ ∪ ∁, sufficient and necessary conditions, quadratic, fractional and absolute-value inequalities, the basic inequality.

  2. 2Functions函数

    Domain, odd and even, increasing and decreasing, inverse, exponential and log functions, sin/cos/tan and periods.

  3. 3Trigonometry三角恒等变换与解三角形

    Radians, sin² + cos² = 1, reduction formulas, sum and double-angle formulas, graph shifts, laws of sines and cosines.

  4. 4Sequences数列

    General term, aₙ from Sₙ, arithmetic and geometric sequences and their sums, recurrences, telescoping sums.

  5. 5Derivatives导数

    Rate of change, derivative formulas and rules, tangent lines, increasing and decreasing intervals, maxima and minima.

  6. 6Vectors and complex numbers平面向量与复数

    Vectors in coordinates, length, dot product, angles, perpendicular and parallel vectors; i² = −1, operations, conjugate, modulus.

  7. 7Lines and circles直线与圆

    Slope and angle, equations of a line, parallel and perpendicular lines, distances, circles, a line and a circle, chords and tangents.

  8. 8Ellipse, hyperbola, parabola圆锥曲线

    Each conic from its distance definition: a, b, c, foci, eccentricity, asymptotes, focus and directrix.

  9. 9Solid geometry立体几何

    Prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones and spheres; volume and area; parallel, intersecting and skew lines; coordinates in space.

  10. 10Probability and statistics概率与统计

    Classical probability, complement, mutually exclusive and independent events, mean, median, variance, the normal distribution.
